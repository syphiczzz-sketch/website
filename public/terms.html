Application Terms

Last updated: 10 July 2026

Nature of the opportunity

Northweld Games is currently recruiting collaborators for a revenue-share game project. Applying does not create employment, contractor status, a partnership or an entitlement to payment.

Compensation

There is no salary, hourly pay, upfront payment or guaranteed income. Accepted contributors may receive an agreed percentage of net game profit after release. Any percentage, eligible costs, payment schedule and ownership terms must be documented in a separate written agreement before contribution begins.

Applications

Northweld Games may accept, reject or leave an application unanswered. Applicants must provide accurate information and must be at least 18 years old.

Portfolio material

You confirm that you have permission to share the portfolio links and examples included with your application. Northweld Games does not claim ownership of application material.

Project work

Do not begin production work or transfer source files until responsibilities, intellectual-property terms, revenue share and exit conditions have been agreed in writing.
