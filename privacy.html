Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. Corocat collects only the information needed to provide and improve the learning experience.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address and authentication identifiers.
  • User-generated learning content such as topics, notes, course steps, and whiteboard data.
  • Usage information such as feature activity, progress, and subscription status.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide the app, generate learning paths, save progress, operate payments, prevent abuse, improve features, and communicate support updates.

3. Service Providers

The full app may use services such as Firebase, Stripe, Liveblocks, Supabase, Cloudinary, and AI providers. These services process data needed for their respective features.

4. Security

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We use reasonable safeguards and recommend that users protect their account credentials.

5. Contact

Contact the site operator if you have questions about this policy or your data.
